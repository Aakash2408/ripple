Why Ripple?

⚡ Instant

Fixes appear seconds after you push, not days after a team discovers the break in production.

🌐 Multi-language

Generates correct fixes for TypeScript, Python, Java, Go — matches your consumer's language.

🔍 Auto-discovery

No configuration needed. Ripple finds consumers by scanning your org's repos automatically.

🧠 Gets smarter

Learns your org's dependency graph over time. The more you use it, the better it predicts.

How it works

1

2
